## A · HUNT CHALLENGES

### 1. Colour Hunt — Act 1 · Setup `#FF7F5C`
1. Open the card in the app to see your team's 4 assigned colours.
2. Search the mall for storefronts or displays in each colour.
3. Take at least 2 photos for every colour — 8 photos in total.
4. At least 2 team members must appear in each photo.
5. Photo only — never touch stock, enter a display, or block a shop.
6. Upload all colour photos in the app to complete the card.

### 2. AI Virtual Fitting — Act 2 · The Hunt `#14A79A`
1. Find a display outfit in a shop window that suits your character.
2. Stay outside — do not enter the shop or try anything on.
3. Photograph the outfit and the shopfront from the walkway.
4. Use AI to edit that outfit onto one of your team members.
5. Note the shop name in your submission as proof.
6. Upload the AI image and the shopfront photo in the app.

### 3. Book of Clues — Act 2 · The Hunt `#14A79A`
1. Go to a bookstore and browse only — nothing is bought.
2. Find books whose titles read as a line of your plot.
3. Line the books up in the correct order.
4. Film one member reading the titles dramatically out loud.
5. Keep the aisle clear and never block staff or shoppers.
6. Upload the clip in the app to complete the card.

### 4. Product Props — Act 2 · The Hunt `#14A79A`
1. Find 5 products in the mall that suit your movie.
2. Imagine each one as a prop — an umbrella becomes a sword.
3. Film a short action clip for each of the 5 props.
4. Keep clear of shelves and shoppers, and touch nothing.
5. Filming only — no product is bought or opened.
6. Upload all 5 clips in the app to complete the card.

### 5. Escape the Mall — Act 3 · The Mission `#6C63D9`
1. Open the card in the app to draw your riddle.
2. Read the riddle together — it points to one spot in the mall.
3. Solve it as a team, or ask AI for a hint if you are stuck.
4. Travel to that location and confirm you have the right spot.
5. Film your "we cracked it" reaction at the location.
6. Upload the clip in the app to complete the card.

### 6. Shop Hero Poster — Act 5 · The Cut `#2E9E63`
1. Assign every member a different shop in the mall.
2. The shop decides their hero — sports becomes Speed, tech becomes Cyber.
3. Photograph each member standing outside their storefront.
4. Use AI to turn each photo into that hero's poster.
5. Give every hero a name and tag the shop that inspired them.
6. Upload one poster per member in the app.

## B · AI CREATION

### 7. Team Poster — Act 1 · Setup `#FF7F5C`
1. Agree on your movie title and tagline as a team.
2. Take one group "cast" photo somewhere bright in the mall.
3. Use AI to turn that photo into a movie poster.
4. Add your title onto the poster.
5. Check the title is spelled correctly and clearly readable.
6. Upload the finished poster in the app.

### 8. AI Establishing World — Act 1 · Setup `#FF7F5C`
1. Describe the world your story is set in to your AI tool.
2. Generate a 3–5 second establishing clip of that world.
3. Keep the look bright and cinematic.
4. Regenerate if the clip is dark, blurry or off-story.
5. Save it as the opening shot of your film.
6. Upload the video in the app to complete the card.

### 9. Menu Remix — Act 3 · The Mission `#6C63D9`
1. Find any menu or price board in the mall.
2. Photograph the whole board clearly and readably.
3. Use AI to translate every single item on it.
4. Produce all 3 languages — Malay, English and Chinese.
5. Rebuild it as one clean trilingual menu page.
6. Upload the trilingual page and a reveal clip in the app.

### 10. Box Office Forecast — Act 3 · The Mission `#6C63D9`
1. Imagine your film opening in 5 different cities.
2. Ask AI to invent the ticket sales for each city.
3. Ask AI to build a chart from those numbers.
4. Film a mock press conference revealing the results.
5. Sum up your box office in one spoken line.
6. Upload the chart and the clip in the app.

### 11. AI Action Scene — Act 4 · Action `#E4536B`
1. Picture one epic action beat — a chase, a blast or an escape.
2. Write it out as a detailed AI video prompt.
3. Use an AI text-to-video tool to generate it.
4. Produce a 3–5 second cinematic clip.
5. Check it matches your storyline and stays bright and dynamic.
6. Upload the AI action shot in the app.

### 22. AI Villain Reveal — Act 4 · Action `#E4536B`
1. Decide who or what the villain of your movie is.
2. Write it as a detailed AI image or video prompt.
3. Generate your villain and keep it bright and cinematic.
4. Play the villain on one phone screen for the team to see.
5. Film the team reacting in shock on a second phone.
6. Upload the villain and the reaction clip in the app.

### 12. Caption Card — Act 5 · The Cut `#2E9E63`
1. Choose your best 10-second clip so far.
2. Ask AI for a viral caption and hashtags to match it.
3. Keep the caption fun and on-theme for your movie.
4. Display the caption on screen over the clip.
5. Read it aloud once so it lands with the room.
6. Upload the clip and caption in the app.

## C · VIDEO CRAFT

### 20. Stunt Double — Act 4 · Action `#E4536B`
1. Plan one fake action hit — a punch, a shove or a blast.
2. Stand the two actors far apart, one near the camera and one further away.
3. Line the shot up so the gap disappears from the camera angle only.
4. Never touch each other — the whole trick is that you miss.
5. Film the swing and the reaction together in one take.
6. Upload the clip in the app to complete the card.

### 21. Hero Walk — Act 4 · Action `#E4536B`
1. Pick a wide, bright, uncrowded stretch of the mall.
2. Line the whole team up shoulder to shoulder facing the camera.
3. Walk forward together slowly, straight at the lens, no smiling.
4. Keep the line steady and let no one drift ahead.
5. Film it from low down so the team fills the frame.
6. Upload the clip in the app to complete the card.

### 13. Match Cut — Act 4 · Action `#E4536B`
1. Plan two clips that will connect as one motion.
2. Clip 1: toss an object upward so it leaves the top of frame.
3. Clip 2: "catch" the same object entering frame somewhere else.
4. Line up the direction and speed between the two clips.
5. Keep the toss safe, low and clear of other shoppers.
6. Upload both clips in the app to complete the card.

### 14. Hyperlapse — Act 5 · The Cut `#2E9E63`
1. Pick a bright, open stretch of the mall.
2. Set your phone to hyperlapse or time-lapse mode.
3. Walk steadily forward through the space while filming.
4. Keep the motion smooth and the phone level.
5. Aim for a finished clip of 5–10 seconds.
6. Upload the clip in the app to complete the card.

### 15. B-Roll — Act 5 · The Cut `#2E9E63`
1. Capture 5 ambient "beauty" shots around the mall.
2. Look for reflections, escalators, plants and lights.
3. No team members need to appear in these shots.
4. Stay out of walkways and never block shoppers.
5. Keep every shot between 3 and 5 seconds.
6. Upload all 5 clips in the app.

## D · PLANNING & STRATEGY

### 16. AI Ops Manager — Act 1 · Setup `#FF7F5C`
1. Open your full mission list in the app.
2. Feed every mission to your AI assistant.
3. Ask it to assign each team member a role.
4. Ask it to sequence the missions in the fastest order.
5. Screenshot the finished game plan.
6. Upload the plan and a clip of your director briefing the crew.

### 17. Route Master — Act 2 · The Hunt `#14A79A`
1. Open the card in the app to get your 4 checkpoints.
2. Ask AI for the fastest walking route between them.
3. Follow that route together as one team.
4. Film a short running "chase" clip at every checkpoint.
5. Walk briskly but never run into crowds or block walkways.
6. Upload the route plan and all 4 clips in the app.

### 18. Mission Control Budget — Act 3 · The Mission `#6C63D9`
1. Your production has a pretend budget of RM500.
2. Use an AI builder such as Google AI Studio, Canva AI or Claude Code.
3. Build a simple tool that splits the money across categories.
4. Cover props, cast, effects and marketing, and auto-total it.
5. No real money is ever spent — the budget is fictional.
6. Upload the tool link or screenshot plus a demo clip in the app.

### 19. Craft Services — Act 3 · The Mission `#6C63D9`
1. Set a pretend budget for your wrap-party feast.
2. Read the food court menu boards only — buy nothing.
3. Ask AI to plan a meal for the team that fits the budget.
4. Check every item on the plan really exists in this food court.
5. Film the team hyping up the imaginary feast.
6. Upload the meal plan and clip in the app.
